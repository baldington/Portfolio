PROJECT 02 · EMBEDDED / SENSOR

Battery-Powered
Thermometer

An ESP32-based Tempurature measurement system with a wireless tempurature display

OVERVIEW

Project goal

My parents own a cottage on a roughly one acre plot of land. On said cottage there is a wood-fire smoker barbeque. These barbeques will cook for hours and need wood changing every 30 ± 10 minutes or so. As I don’t want to sit at the fire for hours on end I wanted a probe that could tell me the temperature wirelessly anywhere on the property.

Solution

I designed an ESP32 based probe as its wifi range was roughly big enough without needing any extensions. The board would connect to an external thermistor rated for 100of to 400of that sat in the barbeque. I also designed a 3.7V LiPO battery charging into the board itself so the board would have roughly 2 days worth of battery life and could be charged at any time with a USB-C. I am also currently developing firmware to display the tempurature over wifi.

System

ESP32 · thermistor probe · Power electronics · USB-C charging · wireless data
